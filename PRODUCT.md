## Platform

## Users

Confirmed by the business owner (September 2026, reconfirmed 2026-10-12 for the home cover):
- Primary audience for the home cover: professionals. Distributors and local partners who deploy the technologies in a country or territory; agro-industrial groups, cooperatives and universities that run trials or production on their own land.
- Secondary audience: individual smallholder farmers ("particuliers type petite exploitation"). They come after the professionals, not instead of them.

All are evaluated by first understanding their soil, climate and crop, then matched to a combination of the five technologies and to existing comparable trial results.

## Product Purpose

Green Solutions develops and distributes disruptive agricultural technologies (EVERGREEN®, ECOSORB®, ECOFERT®, Water Vital®, NAPEMA®, and the Paulownia ALTIFOLIA® / agroforestry program) that increase yield and quality while preserving and regenerating the natural resources, chiefly soil and irrigation water, that yield depends on.

The first action wanted from a visitor on the home cover is to ask for a quote ("Faire un devis").

## Positioning

Confirmed by the business owner as a dual, equally weighted differentiator:
- **Verifiable proof over marketing claims**: real third-party lab reports and certifications (SOHISCERT, EU Bio, Polymex acrylamide analysis, SGS REACH/SVHC screening), downloadable, not just asserted.
- **Cost/efficiency ratio**: the technologies are significantly more economical than competing options for a comparable result ("beaucoup plus économiques, le ratio coût efficience est très favorable").
- From site evidence: the five technologies are designed to reinforce one another rather than being sold as unrelated products.

## Operating Context

B2B/B2C hybrid sales motion: first contact (agronomist conversation), matching technologies to the prospect's soil, climate and crop, referencing prior comparable trials, then samples and dosage guidance and, for larger accounts, pilot trials on the partner's own land. The site states 15 pilot trials running in 2026, 30 partners and a footprint in 42 countries (home page figures in `src/lib/content.ts`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Universal applicability claimed on the site: usable on any plant or crop, under any climate, on any soil type.
- Dosage rule on the site: 3 grams per liter of usable soil.
- ECOSORB® and EVERGREEN® are described as non-sodium and free of free acrylamide (ECOSORB® corn-starch based, EVERGREEN® potassium based).
- Biodegradable per the site: useful life 2 to 3 years for ECOSORB®, 3 to 4 years for EVERGREEN®, degrading 20 to 25 percent a year with no accumulation.
- Certified organic-compatible: SOHISCERT and EU Bio, valid through September 4, 2027.
- The site is published in 14 languages (fr, en, es, pt, ar, zh, id, de, it, el, tr, pl, hr, ca); Arabic is right to left. French is the source language. All wording on the cover must exist in every language already, or be added in all fourteen.
- The home cover must keep: navigation to the site's sections, the language switcher, the quote action, the partner pages, the "impact économique par hectare" calculator link, and the five entry points to the technologies (retention-eau, eau-restructuree, fertilisation, protection, paulownia).

## Product Principles

1. Certified, not just announced: every claim ties to a real, checkable document.
2. Field-proven across climates and soils, not lab-only.
3. Zero toxic residue in soil, air or water.
4. The technologies reinforce one another as a system rather than as isolated products.
5. A favorable cost/efficiency ratio against competing solutions.
