## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences, confirmed by the business owner:
- Distributors and local partners who deploy the technologies within a given country/territory.
- Agro-industrial groups, cooperatives, and universities running trials or production use on their own land (the 14 pilot trials listed on the site are this audience).
- Individual smallholder farmers ("particuliers type petite exploitation").

All are evaluated by first understanding their soil, climate, and crop, then matched to a combination of the five technologies and existing comparable trial results.

## Product Purpose

Green Solutions develops and distributes disruptive agricultural technologies (EVERGREEN®, ECOSORB®, ECOFERT®, Water Vital®, NAPEMA®, and the Paulownia ALTIFOLIA® / agroforestry program) that increase yield and quality while preserving and regenerating the natural resources — chiefly soil and irrigation water — that yield depends on.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the business owner as a dual, equally-weighted differentiator:
- **Verifiable proof over marketing claims**: real third-party lab reports and certifications (SOHISCERT, EU Bio, Polymex acrylamide analysis, SGS REACH/SVHC screening), downloadable, not just asserted.
- **Cost/efficiency ratio**: the technologies are significantly more economical than competing options for a comparable result — a differentiator the owner called out explicitly ("beaucoup plus économiques, le ratio coût efficience est très favorable").
- Additionally (from site evidence): the five technologies are designed to reinforce one another rather than being sold as unrelated, standalone products.

## Operating Context

B2B/B2C hybrid sales motion: first contact (agronomist conversation), matching technologies to the prospect's soil/climate/crop, referencing prior comparable trials, then samples/dosage guidance and, for larger accounts, pilot trials on the partner's own land. 14 such pilot trials are currently running (as of September 2026) across partner groups, cooperatives, and universities in a 30-country network.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Universal applicability: all disruptive solutions are usable on any plant/crop, under any climate, and on any soil type (explicit site claim).
- Dosage rule: 3 grams per liter of usable soil, consistent across crops and climates.
- ECOSORB® and EVERGREEN® are potassium-based, not sodium- or acrylamide-based — compatible with food crops and soil microfauna, unlike competing sodium/acrylamide hydrogels.
- Biodegradable: ECOSORB® 2–3 years useful life, EVERGREEN® 3–4 years, degrading 20–25%/year with no accumulation or overdose risk.
- Certified organic-compatible: SOHISCERT / EU Bio, valid through September 4, 2027.

## Product Principles

1. Certified, not just announced — every claim ties to a real, checkable document.
2. Field-proven across climates and soils, not lab-only.
3. Zero toxic residue in soil, air, or water.
4. The technologies are designed as a reinforcing system, not isolated products.
5. Favorable cost/efficiency ratio versus competing solutions.
